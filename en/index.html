Why Zapio

Three things an IPTV player has to get right.

  • Change channels like on a real TV.

    A still frame instead of black: for channels you have watched before, the last frame appears instantly and stays until the live picture takes over.

  • Everything in its place.

    A Home screen with “On TV Now” and Continue Watching, a guide across several days, one search for channels, movies, series and shows. Channel names without technical suffixes.

  • No account. No subscription.
    No tracking.

    Your login details stay in your device’s keychain. Zapio has no server of its own, no ads and no analytics. Buy once, done.

One stream, no stress

Zapio knows your provider’s rules.

Many accounts allow only one stream at a time. Zapio releases streams cleanly, waits after a switch until the provider frees the old connection, and tells you plainly when another device is watching.

  • One at a time.

    Live, movie and catch-up share the one stream – never two at once.

  • Switch devices, stream released.

    Pause and leave the app, and Zapio releases the stream shortly after – so the Apple TV in the living room can take over right away.

  • Private Relay and VPN detected.

    If the provider blocks because of iCloud Private Relay or a VPN, Zapio names the cause and explains the fix.

  • Self-test in Diagnostics.

    Account, network, Apple player and VLC are checked one by one – with a clear result.

Family

    Swipe up from the bottom edge and the picture keeps playing small – with the Apple player.

  • AirPlay

    Live channels and movies on Apple TV or an AirPlay TV – with the Apple player.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    “Turn on … in Zapio”, “What’s on tonight in Zapio”, “Continue watching in Zapio”.

  • “On TV Now” widget

    Small, medium or large on the Home Screen. Tap to tune in.

  • Lock Screen controls

    Pause, resume and change channels from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

  • Multiple sources

    Playlists and Xtream accounts side by side – each with its own favorites, progress and watched marks.

  • Built-in VLC player

    MKV, AVI, MPEG‑TS and DASH play anyway – Zapio switches on its own and remembers it per channel.

  • Filter languages

    Providers often mix dozens of languages. Zapio recognizes them – even from country names in categories. Pick yours; the rest disappears.

  • Multiview

  • Live events

    Event and pay‑per‑view channels show what’s on when. Instead of a black screen you see “Starts at 8:30 PM”.

  • Straight answers

    If your provider sends no picture, only black, or its server is down, Zapio tells you right away – and that it’s not the app.

  • On TV – here without ads

    When a movie is airing on TV that your provider also has as a movie, Zapio shows it to you – without commercial breaks.

  • Tonight at prime time

    What’s on tonight, right on the Home screen – and last night’s shows to catch up on in the archive.

  • New episodes

    A notification as soon as your series has a new episode. Reminders five minutes before a show starts.

  • Favorites tab

    Your channels, movies and series in one place – the same on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV via iCloud.

  • Learns as you watch

    If a channel starts too slowly in the Apple player, Zapio goes straight to VLC next time. No settings needed.

  • In 8 languages

    English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Turkish.

  • Your history, your call

    Remove single items from “Recently watched” or clear your whole history – on all your devices.

Sources and devices

Your playlist.
On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Zapio is an IPTV player for the playlists and accounts you already have. The app connects straight to your provider – no detour through a Zapio server.

  • M3U and M3U8 playlists.

    By link, or on iPhone and iPad as a file from the Files app. Several playlists side by side, each with its own favorites.

  • Xtream Codes accounts.

    Enter server, user name and password: Zapio loads live channels, movies, series and – if your provider has one – the catch-up archive.

  • EPG in XMLTV format.

    Guide data from your provider or an XMLTV address of your own, compressed (.gz) too. A guide across several days, reminders and search in the guide.

  • Your own cable and satellite TV.

    Zapio finds a Fritz!Box Cable or SAT>IP server on your home network and sets up the channel list with one tap. For Jellyfin, Plex, Emby and TVHeadend, a short guide shows where the channel list (M3U) and guide (XMLTV) are. Everything stays on your home network.

  • iPhone and iPad.

    Swipe to change channels, Picture in Picture, AirPlay and a widget. Multiview with two channels on iPhone and up to four on iPad – if your account allows several streams.

  • Apple TV.

    Change channels with the Siri Remote, a grid guide, Continue Watching in the Top Shelf. Take over accounts from your iPhone – with a code on your own network or through iCloud Keychain.

  • One purchase for every device.

    The full version works on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV with the same Apple Account. iCloud syncs favorites and Continue Watching – without login details.

Questions

Short answers.

Does Zapio come with channels?

Only for trying it out. Zapio is a player and does not offer any channels itself. For your own content you need a playlist (M3U) or a provider account (Xtream). The built-in demo has seven made-up channels with public test streams and four open movies.

How do I add my account?

Paste a playlist link or enter server, user name and password – Zapio detects the type. On iPhone and iPad you can also open a playlist file from the Files app. Your login details stay in your device’s keychain.

What does Zapio cost?

The demo is always free. With your provider, you get a 7‑day free trial; after that the full version is a one-time purchase in the App Store – no subscription. The App Store shows the price.

My account allows only one stream. Does that work?

Yes, Zapio is built for that. Live, movie and catch-up share the one connection, and when switching, the app waits until the provider frees the old one. If something is playing on another device, Zapio tells you.

Do Picture in Picture and AirPlay work?

Yes, with the Apple player – that is, live channels (HLS) and movies in MP4. Formats like MKV play through the built-in VLC player, which has no Picture in Picture or AirPlay. If a movie also exists as MP4, Zapio picks that version first.

Can I watch shows I missed?

If your provider offers an archive, yes: missed shows per channel, “From the Beginning” in the player, and upcoming shows can be saved for later. How far back the archive goes is up to the provider.

Does Zapio collect data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. The privacy policy lists every server the app contacts. Poster info and the automatic network check can be switched off.

Can I use Zapio as an M3U player on Apple TV?

Yes. Zapio also runs on Apple TV (tvOS 17 or later). Enter a playlist link or Xtream account there directly, or take it over from your iPhone – with a code on the same network or through iCloud Keychain. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Do Xtream Codes work on iPhone?

Yes. Enter your provider’s server, user name and password – Zapio loads live channels, movies, series, the guide and, if your provider has one, the catch-up archive.

Does Zapio show an EPG on iPad?

Yes. The guide shows your provider’s programme data (XMLTV) across several days – on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. If the guide is missing or incomplete, add an XMLTV address of your own, compressed (.gz) works too.

Can I watch several channels at once?

Yes, with Multiview: two or four channels on iPad and Apple TV, two on iPhone. Each channel is a stream of its own, so Multiview is only available if your account allows several streams at the same time.

Which devices does Zapio run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and Apple TV with tvOS 17 or later – in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Turkish.
