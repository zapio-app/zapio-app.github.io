Privacy Policy for Zapio

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Controller

Andreas Klundt, Rieheweg 1, 32469 Petershagen, zapio.app@proton.me

In short

Zapio has no account and no server of its own. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no automatic crash reports sent to us. Everything you enter or save in the app stays on your device. To work, the app connects directly to the services listed below. Data only reaches us if you send us a support email yourself (see "Support requests by email").

What is stored on your device

  • In the iOS keychain: the address of your playlist or your Xtream login (server, username, password), the address of the TV guide, the kids mode PIN and the state of the trial and purchase. These entries are bound to this device and do not move to a new device.
  • In the app's catalog database: channel lists, movies, series, TV guide data, favorites, viewing progress and cached information from TMDB. This includes the stream addresses of all channels and titles. For Xtream logins these addresses contain your username and password, because the providers' format requires it. The database lives only in the app's protected area (its "sandbox", folder Application Support) and is encrypted by iOS data protection while the device has not been unlocked since it was restarted.
  • In the app's settings: your settings, search history, reminders, saved programmes, hidden languages and locked categories.
  • As files: downloads of movies and episodes, still images of recently watched channels (in the cache, which iOS may clear on its own) and a playback log for troubleshooting (at most about 200 KB, without username and password; it may contain the shortened public IP address from the network check, e.g. "93.184.x.x"). In addition, summaries of the crash and hang reports that iOS records on the device and hands to the app (MetricKit) are kept in the files diagnostics.log and diagnostics-last.json (together at most about 1 MB): time, app build, iOS version, device type, exception type and code or hang duration and – only in diagnostics-last.json – the technical call stack of the latest report, without login data.

Zapio does not transmit this data to us.

Backups and deletion

Like with other apps, the catalog database, the settings, the playback log and the crash and hang reports (diagnostics.log, diagnostics-last.json) are part of your device backup (iCloud backup or a backup on your computer) – and with them the stream addresses that contain the Xtream login. Apple encrypts iCloud backups; for backups on a computer we recommend the option "Encrypt local backup". Downloads and still images are excluded from backups.

Everything stays until you delete it: when you remove a source in Settings, the app deletes its catalog with all stream addresses, its TV guide, its login, saved programmes and downloads. Favorites and progress (only the IDs of the titles, without addresses) are kept so that they are back when you set up the same source again. You can clear the TMDB cache under Settings → "Clear Cache". The app deletes past TV guide data on its own. If you delete the app, iOS removes all of its data.

Who the app talks to

Every server the app connects to sees your IP address, the time and technical details of the request, as with any internet connection. Zapio sends none of these services identifiers of your device or information about you.

  • Your provider (the servers whose address you enter): when loading the channel list, catalog and TV guide, and during playback. Your login and the chosen channels and titles are transmitted. Purpose: the playback you set up yourself. The same applies to a custom TV guide address you enter.
  • Images from your list: Zapio loads channel logos and posters from the addresses in your list. Only the request for the image is transmitted.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB), api.themoviedb.org and image.tmdb.org: when rows of movies and series are shown (for each visible poster) and when you open a movie or series page. The title and, if known, the year of the movie or series and the app's language are transmitted. Posters and images come from image.tmdb.org. Can be turned off under Settings → "Load Posters and Info".
  • iptv-org (iptv-org.github.io, GitHub Pages): at most once a month the public channel database, if your provider is missing channel logos, and the free channel list for "Load more free channels (iptv-org)". The logos themselves come from the image addresses listed in that database. Only the request is transmitted.
  • Demo, free channels and open movies: the channel list and TV guide of the demo ("Try with free channels") are inside the app. Its test streams come from Apple (devstreaming-cdn.apple.com) and Mux (test-streams.mux.dev). The demo and the iptv-org list both include the open movies of the Blender Foundation: they come from download.blender.org and upload.wikimedia.org, and their posters from upload.wikimedia.org – the app loads the posters as soon as the demo or the iptv-org list is shown. After "Load more free channels (iptv-org)", the channels' streams come directly from the broadcasters' servers (their addresses are in the public iptv-org list). Only the request for the chosen channel, movie or image is transmitted.
  • api.ipify.org: in the playback self-test (Settings → "Diagnostics"), for "Check network" in Help, and automatically at most once per app launch when your provider blocks a channel switch. You can turn the automatic check off under Settings → "Check network automatically when blocked". The app briefly asks three times for its public IP address (once unencrypted, twice encrypted). If the answers differ, iCloud Private Relay is active or the address keeps changing – the app then explains what helps. The answer contains only the IP address; Zapio shows it shortened and sends it nowhere.
  • YouTube: if you tap "Trailer", the video opens in the YouTube app or in the browser. Google's privacy policy applies there.
  • Apple: the trial and purchase go through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles the payment; Zapio only learns whether the trial and the full version are active. Siri, Spotlight search, widgets and reminders use iOS features on your device.

TMDB, GitHub, ipify, Apple (test streams), Mux, the Blender Foundation, Wikimedia and the servers of the free channels receive no login data and nothing about your channel list. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

Transfers to third countries (USA)

Some of the services named above are based or have their servers outside the EU, mainly in the USA: TMDB (api.themoviedb.org, image.tmdb.org), GitHub (iptv-org.github.io), ipify (api.ipify.org), the Wikimedia Foundation (upload.wikimedia.org), Apple (devstreaming-cdn.apple.com), Mux (test-streams.mux.dev) and Google (YouTube). Where your provider, a custom TV guide address or the servers of the free channels are located depends on your choice; they can be anywhere in the world. With these connections, the service receives your IP address and the information listed in the section above.

If a service is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (see www.dataprivacyframework.gov), the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). For the other services there is no such decision and no contract between us and the service: your device connects directly, and only when you use the respective function. In the USA, authorities may be able to access data without you having the same legal remedies as in the EU. If you do not want this, turn off TMDB and the automatic network check in Settings and do not use trailers or the free channels.

Legal bases

  • Connections to your provider, to the free channels and open movies, to iptv-org and to Apple serve the functions you use in the app: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Requests to TMDB (posters and info) and to api.ipify.org (network check) are based on our legitimate interest in showing titles in an understandable way and explaining playback problems: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can turn off TMDB and the automatic network check in Settings; the self-test and "Check network" only run when you start them.
  • Support emails: see below.

Support requests by email

"Contact Support" (Settings → "Legal & Contact") opens a prepared email in your own mail app. Besides your text it contains: app version, iOS version, device model, language, the type of your source (Xtream, M3U or free channels) and the number of saved sources, whether the full version is purchased and whether the trial is running, up to five summaries of the latest crashes and hangs (time, app build, iOS version, device type, exception type and code or hang duration) and the latest 20 playback events (for example channel and movie titles, the name of the provider's server, error codes and possibly the shortened IP address). Username and password are removed, and the address of your source is not included.

You see the email before sending, can change or delete anything, and decide yourself whether to send it. It is sent through your own email provider. The same applies if you share the self-test result under "Diagnostics"; it additionally contains your shortened IP address and up to ten summaries of crashes and hangs. With "Share Reports" under "Crashes and Hangs" you share the summaries and the latest full report from iOS (technical call stack, without login data).

If you send us an email, we receive your email address, your text and the diagnostics. We use them only to answer your request and to fix the problem. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (your request about the app) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in fixing errors in the app). We delete the email once your request is resolved, at the latest twelve months after the last contact, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

Children

Kids mode limits what is shown and removes Zapio's entries from the iPhone's Spotlight search while it is on. It collects no data about children.

Your rights

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. In the app you can do this without contacting us: turning off Settings → "Load Posters and Info" stops all requests to TMDB, and turning off Settings → "Check network automatically when blocked" stops the automatic request to api.ipify.org. The self-test and "Check network" only run when you start them yourself. You can object to the processing of your support email by writing to zapio.app@proton.me.

We store no data about you from the app itself. If you have sent us a support email, you have the right to access, rectification and erasure of that data, to restriction of processing and to data portability, and you can object to the processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Questions: zapio.app@proton.me. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
